Common questions

Search shows "You're offline". Searching for songs and artists needs an internet connection. Your saved covers work offline.

Listen is quiet. Turn up the volume. Tunes play even when the ringer is on silent; sound effects follow the silent switch.

Save to Photos doesn't work. Allow Soulprint to add photos in Settings › Soulprint › Photos.

Where are my covers kept? Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Privacy

Soulprint has no accounts, ads or tracking. Read the privacy policy.
