Privacy Policy

Soulprint: Your Music as Art · Effective 24 October 2026

Soulprint does not collect personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app contains no third-party analytics or advertising code.

What stays on your iPhone

The albums you make, your picks and your settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Searching for music

When you search for a song or artist, the text you type is sent to MusicBrainz, an open music database run by the MetaBrainz Foundation, to find matches and their genres. The request identifies the app (its name, version and a developer contact address), not you. As with any internet request, MusicBrainz receives your device's IP address. Their handling of requests is described in the MetaBrainz privacy policy. The genre information for songs and artists you pick is kept on your device for up to 30 days, so it isn't looked up again.

Photos

If you choose Save to Photos, Soulprint asks permission to add images to your photo library. It cannot read your photos.

Children

Soulprint is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
